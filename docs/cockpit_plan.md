## Intended result

A warm, tactile brain cockpit matching the approved `concepts/cockpit/cockpit-warm-ceramic-v1.png`: dense personal clutter around a clear working console. Preserve the contrast between a busy inner life and a calm outside world. Apply `warm_ceramic_v1`, with deep teal/graphite framing, cream/sage housings, dusty coral brain folds, restrained honey accents, and existing functional action colours on active controls.

The concept governs mood, proportions, and clutter density. Its extra buttons, bandwidth display, tiny TV, and labels do not establish new mechanics. Prioritize the player camera composition over unseen room detail.

- Five dial centres at X -0.48, -0.24, 0, 0.24, 0.48; Y 0.955, Z -0.59. Current radius 0.08 m. Full taped labels need an explicit readability/spacing check, especially Embarrassment.
- Desk currently 1.5×0.55 m; console 1.05×0.42 m at `(0, 0.95, 0.1)`, tilt 18°.
- Left keys, central large button, and interchangeable right slot: joystick, Simon pad, lever, arrow keys. Slot sinks active modules 0.2 m, then raises the replacement; reserve the full motion envelope including tween overshoot.
- Screen frames, controls, dial faces/needles/warnings, and cockpit fists/arms are generated by scripts. Decorative instancing alone will not replace those generated meshes. Claude needs agreed art hooks while retaining behavior and input targets.
- Cockpit brain hands are separate from the human-world body/hand system. BrainHand currently uses a procedural fist, knuckles, arm stretch, slam squash, and grip targets. Articulated fingers require a separate pose/rig contract.

## Production sequence and review gates

1. **Camera composition blockout.** In the assets preview only, place simple masses for desk/console, three monitor housings, gauge rail and paper labels, brain enclosure, and representative clutter clusters. Match the current camera and live-screen dimensions. Use placeholder screen content and control motion envelopes. Review menu and gameplay framing at 16:9 and a narrower supported view. Deliver a screenshot for layout review before final detailing.
2. **Core cockpit kit.** Model rounded desk/console panels, monitor housings, gauge bezels/rail, cable attachment points, and broad brain folds. Keep housing openings and live-screen planes distinct. Gauges get full names on separate taped paper strips beneath each dial: Coordination, Confidence, Balance, Focus, Embarrassment. Needles, warning lenses, and moving control pieces stay separable. Review the core in neutral Godot lighting.
3. **Functional control art.** Deliver keys/caps, button cap/base, joystick shaft/knob/base, lever arm/knob/housing, and Simon/arrow modules to Claude's confirmed pivots/contact points. Reserve space for press, tilt, lever pull, module sinking/rising, and the hand sweep. Claude keeps inputs, cues/emission, module swaps, needle motion, and reset behavior. Integrate one module before expanding the set.
4. **Personal clutter kit and dressing.** Build reusable books, mug/can, snack, notebook, loose/crumpled papers, taped notes/photo, small keepsake/mascot, cable bundles, and light housings. Cluster books/notes to the left, mug/keepsakes to the right, notebook/papers along the foreground, and cables/brain folds around the perimeter. Vary angles and scale for an inhabited feel. Preserve density without obscuring screen pixels, full gauge labels, controls, or hand paths. Use a small shared atlas only if static illustrations/print materially improve identity; retain task text as runtime content.
5. **Cockpit hands and final integration.** Establish hand proportions, skin/sleeve palette, and relaxed/press/fist/grip poses after console placement is stable. Recommend one reusable articulated hand rig mirrored for left/right, subject to Claude's pose interface; runtime locomotion/pose selection remains gameplay-owned. Then tune scene lighting, screen contrast, clutter placement, and performance in menu, loading, gameplay, swaps, warning states, and reveal transitions.

First deliverable is the composition blockout, not a fully decorated room. Each later handoff is a small recoverable batch. Hand modelling is part of the cockpit goal, but is not bundled into the initial shell handoff.

User approved reusing free public-domain assets where appropriate. Four Kenney CC0 props are adapted in the blockout; verified sources, selected originals, licences and adaptations are recorded in `free_asset_sources.md` and `../source/vendor/kenney/sources.json`. Custom housings/controls still follow the technical brief.
